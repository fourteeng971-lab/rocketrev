Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Rocket Rev is a game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: Rocket Rev does not collect personal data.

Information we collect

None. Rocket Rev has no accounts or sign-in. It does not include analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (cash, gems, upgrades, unlocked cars and worlds, cosmetics, daily reward progress) and your sound and haptics settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see or access them. They may be included in your device backups, according to your own backup settings. Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The game checks which items you own using Apple's StoreKit on your device. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because Rocket Rev does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Support requests

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, what you write is posted publicly on GitHub and handled under GitHub's Privacy Statement. Please do not include personal information in an issue.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding a feature that sends data off your device, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/fourteeng971-lab/rocketrev/issues.
