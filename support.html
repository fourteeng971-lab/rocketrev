Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us if you need more help.

Playing

How do I launch?

Drag down anywhere on the launch screen to pull the car back in the slingshot. Move sideways to aim, then let go. Pull farther for more power. To cancel, return your finger to where you started.

How do I get a perfect launch?

Pull all the way down to rev the sling. The power gauge surges up and down; release while it is in the gold zone at the top for a perfect launch and 15% more cash.

Can I launch without dragging?

Yes. Tap the ? button in the garage to open the Launch guide, which has sliders for power and angle, a launch button, and a timed release for perfect launches. VoiceOver and Switch Control users can also launch from the slingshot's accessibility actions.

How do I unlock the next car?

Reach your newest car's evolve distance, shown in the garage, then tap Evolve. Earlier cars do not count toward evolution. Each car has its own upgrades, so a new car starts stock.

How do I restart a run?

Tap the ↺ button next to pause, or pause and choose Restart. The current run ends without a payout.

Daily rewards

When does the next reward unlock?

At midnight in your device's time zone. The rewards screen shows a countdown. Missing a day never resets your progress; you simply continue from the next day.

Purchases

What do purchases include?

Purchases are cosmetic only: paints, trails, tire smoke and vehicle bodies, plus gem packs to spend on cosmetics. Style packs apply to the car named in the pack. Nothing you can buy changes speed or distance.

How do I restore purchases on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Custom Shop from the garage and tap Restore Purchases. Style packs are restored. Gem packs are consumable, so gems already added to your balance are not restored.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account to request a refund.

Saving and settings

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Finished runs save automatically. Progress does not sync between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress unless it is included in a device backup you restore.

How do I change the language?

Rocket Rev follows your device language. To choose a different language for the game only, open the Settings app, go to Apps › Rocket Rev › Language.

There is no sound.

Check the volume sliders in the game's Settings (gear button) and whether Mute in Silent Mode is on while your device is in Silent Mode.

Still need help?

Open an issue at github.com/fourteeng971-lab/rocketrev/issues. Tell us your device model, iOS version, the app version (shown in the game's Settings) and what happened. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.

Rocket Rev runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.
